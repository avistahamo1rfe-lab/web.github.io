About

Mara Voss writes essays about attention, memory, and the ordinary textures of daily life. Her work has appeared in a handful of small magazines and a great many notebooks that never left the house.

She is currently at work on a collection about the correspondence her family kept during the years they lived apart, and what it means to write toward someone instead of at an audience.
